# Datasheet — Experiment 1 records

Following the *Datasheets for Datasets* structure. Describes
`data/records/tier1_records.jsonl` and its Tier-2/3 companion.

## Motivation

**Why was this created?** To test whether a language model holds a run-specific commitment
before it is asked to report one — the deferred-commitment misattribution of *From Deceptive
Outputs to Deceptive Mechanisms*. Existing work infers prior commitments from later reports;
this records the model's full candidate distribution *before* any token is selected, so the
inference can be checked rather than assumed.

**Who created it?** Yakov P. Shkolnikov. No external funding.

## Composition

Each record is one **probe**: a candidate distribution measured at one captured model state,
under one condition and one readout prompt. Records never contain a sampled answer presented
as a measurement — sampling is recorded separately, with its seed and sampler role.

| | |
|---|---|
| Instances | 2,400 Tier-1 probes, 5,760 Tier-2/3 probes, 960 bare-value probes, across 2 models |
| Fields | 51, schema `probe_record.v1`, validated in both QA stacks |
| Models | `mlx-community/gemma-4-26B-A4B-it-qat-8bit`; `sprisa/Qwen3.6-27B-MLX-8bit-MTP` |
| Conditions | C0 commitment framing · C1 target injected · C2 target + reason · C4 distractor (Tier 1); C3 constraint-only · C5 prior-only (Tier 2) |
| Design | 40 games × 2 probe points × 3 readout paraphrases, plus 240 branch states at R = 200 |
| Bare-value arm | 3 conditions × 40 games × 2 probe points × 2 readouts, scored over all 100 fixed-width values by prefix trie |
| Turn-resolved arm | 4 conditions × 20 games × 4 intermediate probe points × 3 paraphrases; the `early` and `late` end points are the Tier-1 measurements, joined rather than re-measured |

**Is anything missing?** Yes, by design. Full float32 logit vectors are **not** stored for
any probe — 262,144 floats per probe would be roughly 30 TB. Each record instead carries a
BLAKE2b digest of the full vector, the candidate subset, the top-64 tokens, and the
normalisation constant, which together permit independent re-verification.

**Does it contain confidential or personal data?** No. Scenarios are synthetic: ten integers
drawn from 0–99 and yes/no questions about them. No human subjects, no personal data, no
scraped content.

**Are there errors or noise?** Three known limitations, each recorded in the data rather than
corrected away:

- `candidate_mass` is reported per probe and is **never** used to filter. A low value means
  the model would rather not answer with a candidate at all, which is information about the
  model, not a defect to be excluded.
- `G3` (constraint tracking) fails for both models. See `results/FINDINGS.md` F5 — the
  threshold was mis-specified against a no-reasoning elicitation, and is deliberately not
  being moved after the fact.
- Candidates are single-token **indices**, which are pointers, so part of what the readout
  measures is positional preference rather than target content (F6). The bare-value records
  are the check on that, and they are the ones to read before generalising any index-arm
  number to "what the model prefers".
- Divergence must be read **per condition**. Pooling C0 with C1/C2 averages conditions
  designed to differ.

## Collection

Generated by a local MLX harness on Apple Silicon; nothing was collected from third parties
or the network. Weights were loaded read-only from a local cache with `HF_HUB_OFFLINE=1`,
pinned by revision SHA and weight digest, both recorded in every manifest.

Every pre-reveal assistant turn is **written by the harness from a fixed script**, so the captured state is identical
across branches by construction rather than by sampling stability. The full logit vector is
recorded and digested **before** any RNG is initialised.

Collection ran 2026-08-31 to 2026-09-01. Every shipped record comes from a **single run** of
the corrected instrument, and every record and manifest carries the same pipeline
fingerprint.

Two classes of earlier run are retained and excluded from every analysis.
`tier1_records_INVALID_*.jsonl` are runs made before the instrument was working; the faults
are documented in `APPENDIX_TECHNICAL.md`. `*_SUPERSEDED_pre_review.jsonl` are runs of a
working instrument that an adversarial review then corrected — chiefly C2, which was
injecting reasons that were false of their own targets. They are retained because the
difference between them and the shipped records is itself inspectable.

## Preprocessing

None between measurement and shipping. Records are written as measured. All derived
quantities — entropy, divergence, agreement, effect sizes — are computed in the analysis
package from the shipped fields, so every number in the paper is recomputable from what is
here.

## Uses

**Appropriate.** Verifying the Tier-1 claims; recomputing any reported statistic;
re-analysing under different metrics; comparing candidate distributions across conditions.

**Inappropriate, and stated because the shape of the data invites it:**

- **Do not read a low `candidate_mass` probe's conditional distribution as the model's
  answer.** It is a conditional on a rare event.
- **Do not pool models.** Gemma and Qwen differ sharply on distractor capture (F10), and
  Gemma is QAT while Qwen is post-training quantised — family is confounded with
  quantisation method.
- **Do not treat these records as evidence about belief representations generally.** They
  measure one elicitation at the logit level. C1 shows the models *can* hold a supplied
  target; nothing here bears on representations not surfaced by this probe.
- **Do not read a Tier-2 or Tier-3 result as bearing on Tier 1.** Every record carries its
  tier for this reason.
- **Do not read a two-point drift number as Luo et al.'s Drift Rate.** Two probe points give
  their Appendix C Branch Drift Rate, `1(p_T ≠ p_0)`, which is a lower bound on how often the
  target changed and is silent about when. The turn-resolved records carry the six-point
  trajectory their headline metric needs.
- **Do not compare a bare-value record to an index record state-for-state.** They are
  different transcripts producing different states in different answer spaces. The arm is a
  robustness check on whether the phenomenon survives, not a paired contrast.

## Distribution and maintenance

MIT, with the replication package. Prompt provenance in `prompts/LICENSING.md`. Third-party
bibliography PDFs are deliberately not redistributed.

The schema is versioned. Fields reserved for Experiments 2–4 are present and nullable, so
those experiments will not require a migration that would break comparability with these
records.
